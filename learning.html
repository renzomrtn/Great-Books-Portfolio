Summary of Learning

My Learnings

I learned in Great Books how to sharpen my skills in close reading and critical analysis. There's no one true way of interpreting a text- the lenses helped me see different perspectives. I like to think of it as a different reading glass or even a microscope. I can look at the same literature but with a different perspective. Considering different aspects such as idea behind it, the response of the reader, or why a character did this or did that. It also even more broaden my mind in understanding different contexts such as history and culture through the books in this course.

It helped me see more than just texts on a paper because it emphazised the importance of close attention to imagery narration, and structure. The "big ideas" behind the texts that helps shapes our understanding of society and human nature is even more realized through this course because it encourages us to think deeper and question even ourselves and compare it with different literature while also utilizing the different lenses, understaning the elements, and considering the contexts and drivers of these characters by having an open mind.

Open mindedness is something that this course greatly enhanced about me. Because I was exposed to different perspectives and ways of thinking, I became more open minded to different ideas and more willing to consider alternative viewpoints- which made me really appreciate the literature even more.
