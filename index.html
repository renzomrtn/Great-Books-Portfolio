Reading life
Likes Poetry · Retired Wattpad reader · Enjoys Science Fiction and Fantasy

I started having interest in reading during my early teenage years. It started when I saw a lot of hidden books in my household and was immediately drawn to their stories such as The Black Cat by John Milne, and Tales of Goha by Leslie Caplan. I gathered these books and would read them daily until i finish them all- then read them again. But in my highschool years, when I was introduced to Wattpad, I started reading more stories and novels online. I would read stories that are trending and popular, and also my favorite genres and would read them daily. I even got a physical book of some of the stories I liked. Though, I would say that my reading life is not as active as it was before.
