My favorite literary masterpiece

The Tale of Genji

Murasaki Shikibu

Brief overview

Genji is the handsome son of an emperor and a low-ranking concubine (Kiritsubo). Because he lacks strong political backing and to protect the crown prince's succession, the emperor strips him of royal status and makes him a commoner under the surname Minamoto (Genji)

Why it's my favorite

The first thing that got my attention in this story is the the hardship that Genji experienced, epsecially coming from his own family. It is something that I recognize. But what I really like about it is how it explores the complexities of human relationships and the impact of social structures. It doesn't only focus on Genji and his oedipus complex. It also highlights things that we can all relate to and things we should talk about. It talked about gender inequality, seen in Genji's mother's point of view, the social class and treatment of people, how Genji's own family treated him, and it also talks about mental health- as seen with Genji's feeling of emptiness and his coping mechanisms of trying to find love in something or someone- A response due to a traumatic experience. It also made me realize that regardless of the generation, negativity will coninue to exist as these problems and hardship help us get better- or even make us give up. Especially for me who currently undergoes self-healing and aims to be better mentally and emotionally. Through the story, I realized that these negativities happens to everyone and that we can only learn to overcome it rather than giving into it.
