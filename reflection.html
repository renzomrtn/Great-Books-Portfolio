Reflections on the Great Books Experience

At the start, I already thought that the subject was gonna be about old literature and analysis and stuff. I'm not really that much of a fan of old literature so I thought it might bore me. Although I do think that the idea of analysing literature is fun and meaningful- I just didn't want to do that with old books. But my mind was changed instantly. I realized that they are called Great Books for a reason and that reason is our fuel why we study them. In the end, I wasn't really bored but rather informed and actually had fun analyzing and interpreting. Througought the course, I valued and appreacted these masterpieces more.

I can say that I think only the challenging thing for me in this course was having the time to read them. Because most of the masterpieces that I have an analysis of are all very long. Sometimes, even understanding the story and their complexity is another factor too. But I managed to overcome these challenges by finding time and having more research, even watching them via YouTube to gain more information about it and understand it further.

In terms of influence about interpretation of literature, I cand definitely say that Psychoanalytic and Reader-Response lens influenced me the most. Both these lenses are very relatable to me and I can understand them more. I can relate to the characters, what their motivations are and how they develop throughout the story, and I can also have my different perspectives- As someone who constantly overthinks even the simplest stuff in life, I definitely appreciate Reader-Response lens.

About realizations in life, society, culture, or human nature, I definitely gained a deeper understanding of humanity's tendencies, drivers and motivation, values, and how society and culture affects them- and in return how they affect society and culture- Like a loop. Everything interconnected and everything affects each other, even time in terms of new generations.

Overall, this course has been a really enriching experience for me as I have gained a lot of knowledge and insight, and really made me be more open minded. Because we can't just apply open-mindedness in books. Even the happenings in those books, happens somewhere and to someone. It's only a matter of perspective. So I will apply these lessons by keeping an open mind in the real world. But most importantly, I can take these great values that these legendary characters represent and apply them my own life, trying to be better everyday, basically bringing them into reality.
